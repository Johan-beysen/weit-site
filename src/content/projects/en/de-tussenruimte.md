## How it started

Liesbeth and I already knew each other - she had helped me on my way toward a new career, I had helped her think through hers. When she decided to go freelance as a career coach and needed a website, the deal was made quickly.

Because she had helped me out earlier, I built her website with pleasure - a way of giving something back.

## Approach

First question I asked her: look around the web, give me an idea of what appeals to you. Colors, fonts, navigation, structure - just what feels right. Based on that input I built a proof of concept. It landed immediately - colors, layout, the feel. Everything clicked from the start.

Then waited for the photographer. Got the final photos and texts, implemented them, polished here and there, and we were done.

## What was built

Fully hand-written HTML, CSS and JavaScript - no website builders, no themes, no shortcuts. A static site that loads fast and is easy to maintain.

| Component | What it does |
|-----------|------|
| Blog | So Liesbeth can write relevant articles herself - good for discoverability. |
| Decap CMS | Admin portal for the blog, expanded to manage the full site content. |
| Calendly | Integration for appointments - book directly without back-and-forth emails. |
| Google Tag Manager | Conversion tracking for Google Ads campaigns, with an open source cookie consent banner. |
| Security headers | A+ score on CSP and security headers. Privacy policy and robots.txt included. |
| Publishing | GitHub → Netlify. Mailboxes created and configured. |

## More than a website

Liesbeth started from zero digitally. No password management, no good password hygiene. That was addressed too: Proton Pass installed and taught, credentials handed over properly.

Everything lives on her own accounts - DNS, Netlify, GitHub, mailboxes. She owns every line of code and every login. If I still make changes to her site, it's because she wants that - not because there's no other way.

That's the norm for me, not the exception.
